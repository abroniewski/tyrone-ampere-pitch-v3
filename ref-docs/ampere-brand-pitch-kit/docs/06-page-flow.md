# 06. Page Flow

## Current public information architecture

The indexed official site uses this top-level structure:

1. Home
2. About Us
3. Approach
4. Portfolio
5. Insights
6. Careers
7. Contact

The included page shells follow that architecture while using original pitch content.

## Homepage flow

### 1. Hero statement

Purpose: establish the promise in one visual strike.

Content:

- Four-line headline.
- Two-sentence support block.
- One secondary action.
- Hero imagery or motion field.

### 2. Opportunity or manifesto

Purpose: translate the opening statement into a business idea.

Content:

- One large statement.
- Two short paragraphs.
- Optional section number.

### 3. Brand rhythm interruption

Purpose: inject pace between narrative sections.

Use a horizontal marquee, partner strip, or compact proof band.

### 4. Strategic system

Purpose: explain the core offer or approach.

Use three numbered cards with one idea per card.

### 5. Campaign, portfolio, or case-study work

Purpose: make the system tangible.

Use an asymmetric image grid. Avoid a uniform gallery.

### 6. Large proof

Purpose: shift from story to evidence.

Use three large stat cards, one case-study result, or a prominent quote.

### 7. Process or rollout

Purpose: remove uncertainty.

Use four timeline rows with direct phase names and concrete outputs.

### 8. Emotional or strategic quote

Purpose: create a final moment of scale before editorial or conversion content.

### 9. Insights

Purpose: show the brand has a point of view and a repeatable content engine.

### 10. Major CTA

Purpose: end with one action on a full cobalt field.

## About page flow

1. Ambition-led hero.
2. Origin and operating story.
3. Pivotal decisions or milestones.
4. Leadership grid.
5. Culture or principles.
6. CTA to partner, join, or contact.

## Approach page flow

1. Clear acquisition or partnership thesis.
2. Three operating principles.
3. Step-by-step process.
4. Proof from a portfolio example.
5. Founder-oriented FAQ or objections.
6. CTA.

## Portfolio page flow

1. Collective-level hero.
2. Portfolio index with large rows.
3. Featured case study.
4. Shared-capability explanation.
5. Performance evidence.
6. Partner CTA.

Each company detail page should use:

1. Company name and sector.
2. Pre-partnership context.
3. Strategic intervention.
4. Measurable results.
5. Leadership quote.
6. Cross-portfolio advantage.
7. Next horizon.

## Insights page flow

1. Editorial point-of-view hero.
2. Featured article.
3. Filterable or grouped insight rows.
4. Newsletter signup.
5. CTA to discuss strategy or partnership.

## Careers page flow

1. Ambition and impact statement.
2. Honest culture narrative.
3. People and workplace evidence.
4. Open-role index.
5. General-interest contact path.
6. CTA.

## Contact page flow

1. Direct invitation.
2. Short expectation-setting paragraph.
3. Minimal form.
4. Optional direct contacts by enquiry type.
5. Office or territory information.
6. Legal and privacy note.

## Flow rule

Alternate between three densities:

- **Declaration**: one large idea and substantial negative space.
- **System**: structured cards, rows, or numbers.
- **Evidence**: imagery, quote, case study, or result.

Repeating the same density twice makes the page feel flat.
