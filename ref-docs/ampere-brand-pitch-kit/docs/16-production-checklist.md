# 16. Production Checklist

## Gate 0: Pitch safety

- Keep `Pitch concept` visible during external review.
- Confirm the presentation is clearly an unofficial proposal.
- Do not present placeholder metrics, quotes, people, or portfolio companies as facts.
- Do not distribute agency case-study screenshots as project assets.

## Gate 1: Brand inputs

- Receive primary, reversed, dark, and symbol-only logo masters.
- Receive favicon and social-avatar files.
- Confirm official color specifications.
- Confirm display and body font families, weights, licenses, and hosting method.
- Receive logo clear-space and minimum-size rules.
- Confirm the current top-level navigation and legal company name.

## Gate 2: Content inputs

- Approve page-level message hierarchy.
- Approve hero headline and one primary conversion action.
- Approve portfolio-company list, sectors, status, locations, and descriptions.
- Verify every statistic, date, and performance claim.
- Approve every attributed quote and job posting.
- Approve privacy, terms, accessibility, and form-consent copy.

## Gate 3: Media inputs

- Receive or produce hero stills and optional loop-safe video.
- Receive approved current portfolio-company logo masters.
- Produce consistent leadership portraits.
- Produce portfolio-company facility, people, detail, and proof imagery.
- Produce culture and careers imagery across roles and geographies.
- Deliver article, Open Graph, and social-image templates.
- Record source, owner, rights, expiry, and restrictions for every asset.

## Gate 4: Design calibration

- Load the approved font before adjusting type size.
- Match hero line breaks at desktop and mobile reference sizes.
- Tune gutters, grid spans, section rhythm, and polygon edges.
- Confirm cobalt, mist, silver, black, and gradient stops.
- Check dark, light, mist, and cobalt themes together.
- Test extra-long names, headlines, role titles, and translations.
- Complete `14-calibration-protocol.md` against current captures.

## Gate 5: Build integration

- Move shared content into a CMS or typed content layer.
- Implement reusable header, hero, card, row, stat, quote, form, CTA, and footer components.
- Use responsive images with explicit dimensions.
- Connect portfolio, insights, people, roles, and testimonials to collections.
- Connect form handling, validation, spam protection, routing, and confirmation email.
- Add canonical URLs, metadata, Open Graph, and structured data.
- Add analytics events based on user intent.
- Add cookie and tracking consent where required.

## Gate 6: Accessibility and performance

- Complete keyboard-only navigation.
- Verify mobile-menu focus management and Escape behavior.
- Verify visible focus on black, cobalt, mist, and white.
- Test VoiceOver and NVDA or JAWS.
- Test reduced motion and high-contrast mode.
- Test 200 percent zoom and reflow.
- Validate form labels, errors, instructions, and status messages.
- Meet the budgets in `11-accessibility-and-performance.md`.
- Test on midrange mobile hardware and a slow network profile.

## Gate 7: Browser and content QA

- Test current Safari, Chrome, Edge, and Firefox.
- Test iOS Safari and Android Chrome.
- Test common widths from 320 to 2560 pixels.
- Verify all internal and external links.
- Verify every image crop and alt attribute.
- Verify dates, punctuation, capitalization, company names, and legal copy.
- Verify no placeholder or concept marker remains unintentionally.
- Verify 404, empty, loading, error, and success states.

## Gate 8: Approval and release

- Obtain brand approval.
- Obtain communications and claims approval.
- Obtain legal and privacy approval.
- Obtain accessibility sign-off.
- Obtain form-routing and analytics sign-off.
- Back up the approved release.
- Remove `Pitch concept` only after approval.
- Monitor errors, form delivery, Core Web Vitals, and conversion events after release.
