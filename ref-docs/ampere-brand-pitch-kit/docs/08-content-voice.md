# 08. Content Voice

## Voice character

The writing should be:

- Ambitious without hype.
- Direct without sounding blunt.
- Industrial without becoming technical sludge.
- Founder-aware without romanticizing entrepreneurship.
- Proof-oriented without reading like a spreadsheet.

## Sentence behavior

Use:

- Short declarations for major headlines.
- Concrete verbs.
- Specific nouns.
- Active voice.
- One strategic idea per paragraph.

Avoid:

- Empty superlatives.
- Generic partnership language.
- Long strings of corporate abstractions.
- Overuse of words such as innovative, leading, world-class, and solutions.
- Copying official page text into a pitch without permission.

## Headline formulas

### Category declaration

`The next generation of [category].`

### Transformation

`From [constraint] to [advantage].`

### Collective advantage

`Stronger [capability]. Smarter [operation]. Together.`

### Proof-led

`[Result], built on [mechanism].`

### Founder-facing

`Scale without losing [valuable quality].`

### Recruiting

`Build what [category] becomes next.`

## Section-copy pattern

A strong section can use:

1. Kicker: `03 / OPERATING ADVANTAGE`
2. Headline: 4 to 10 words.
3. Supporting copy: 25 to 55 words.
4. Proof point: number, name, quote, or result.
5. Action: 2 to 5 words.

## Case-study structure

Use this order:

1. **Context**: what the company had already built.
2. **Constraint**: what was limiting the next stage.
3. **Decision**: what changed.
4. **Mechanism**: how the alliance created leverage.
5. **Result**: measurable operational or commercial impact.
6. **Human proof**: a quote from an operator or founder.
7. **Next horizon**: what is now possible.

## Insight article structure

- Point-of-view title.
- One-sentence thesis.
- Why it matters now.
- Three to five evidence-backed sections.
- Practical implications.
- Clear conclusion or question.

## Social content adaptation

Convert one case study into:

- One 60 to 90 second founder video.
- One number-led static post.
- One three-frame carousel.
- One operator quote.
- One behind-the-scenes image.
- One longer LinkedIn article.

All should share the same headline logic, blue accent, and angular crop system.
