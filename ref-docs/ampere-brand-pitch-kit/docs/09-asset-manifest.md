# 09. Asset Manifest

The machine-readable version is in `data/asset-manifest.json`.

## Official brand assets to request

### Identity

- Primary horizontal logo in SVG.
- Symbol-only logo in SVG.
- Reversed logo.
- Black logo.
- Blue logo, if approved.
- Favicon SVG and PNG set.
- Official brand-color specification.
- Official font names, weights, licenses, and webfont formats. **Live site confirmed General Sans 300/400/500 on 2026-07-16** — see `reference/live-site-extraction.json`.
- Clear-space and minimum-size rules.

### Portfolio identity

Request approved vector logos for every current and historical company that will appear. Likely categories include:

- Current portfolio-company marks.
- Prior portfolio-company marks, when used in case studies.
- Partner, advisor, press, and award marks.

Do not pull low-resolution marks from search results.

## Photography to request or produce

### Hero library

- 6 to 10 horizontal images.
- Minimum 2400 x 1600 pixels.
- People and infrastructure with strong negative space.
- Rights for web, social, presentation, and paid media.

### Leadership

- 4:5 portrait orientation.
- Minimum 1600 x 2000 pixels.
- Consistent lighting and distance.
- Environmental portraits preferred over generic studio headshots.

### Portfolio companies

For each company:

- Exterior or facility establishing image.
- People at work.
- Equipment or product detail.
- Wide operating-environment image.
- One leadership portrait.
- One proof or result image.

### Culture and careers

- Teams collaborating in real environments.
- Apprentices, operators, engineers, and leaders.
- Safety, training, field work, and manufacturing.
- Geographic variety across the operating footprint.

## Video

Recommended hero loop:

- 6 to 10 seconds.
- 1920 x 1080 or higher.
- 24 or 30 frames per second.
- Muted and loop-safe.
- H.264 MP4 plus WebM.
- Target under 8 MB after compression.
- Poster image included.

Recommended interview footage:

- 4K source.
- 16:9 master with safe 9:16 crop.
- Clean audio and captions.
- B-roll of actual operations.

## Editorial and social assets

- Article thumbnails at 1600 x 900.
- Open Graph image at 1200 x 630.
- LinkedIn portrait post at 1200 x 1500.
- LinkedIn landscape post at 1200 x 627.
- Story and reel cover at 1080 x 1920.
- Presentation cover at 1920 x 1080.

## Data and proof assets

For each case study, gather:

- Baseline metric.
- Time period.
- Result metric.
- Source and owner.
- Approval status.
- Short explanation of the mechanism.
- Supporting quote.
- Publication restrictions.

## Optimization formats

For production imagery, create:

- AVIF for modern browsers.
- WebP fallback.
- JPEG fallback only when required.
- Responsive widths at roughly 480, 768, 1200, 1600, and 2400 pixels.
- Meaningful alt text or an empty alt attribute for purely decorative images.
